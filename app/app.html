Model information

The prediction model was trained using cluster-level data from 2017-2021. For each cluster, a representative center point was computed by averaging the geographic locations of all fatal crashes within the cluster. A circular area with a radius of 2000 m was then defined around this center, and the model predicts the expected number of fatal crashes within each such area. The model uses the following input features:

  • fatal_prev_1: number of fatal crashes in the previous year (given only to Structural + past crashes model)
  • fatal_prev_2: number of fatal crashes two years prior (given only to Structural + past crashes model)
  • road_density: density of the road network within each cluster area (given to both models)
  • intersection_density: density of road intersections, representing traffic complexity (given to both models)
  • primary_road_frac: proportion of road segments classified as major arterial roads (motorways, trunk roads, or primary roads) within each cluster, reflecting the dominance of high-capacity, high-speed roads (given to both models)
